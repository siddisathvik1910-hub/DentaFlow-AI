Questions dental teams ask

Will it replace my front-desk team?

No. It takes the repetitive phone work (booking, rescheduling, common questions, reminders) so your team can focus on the patients in front of them. Anything sensitive goes to a person.

What if a patient has an emergency?

If someone describes trouble breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or serious facial swelling or injury, the assistant tells them to call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room and alerts your staff immediately. For urgent dental problems it offers the earliest emergency slot and your on-call number.

Which scheduling systems does it work with?

You can start with the built-in calendar. Connections to practice-management systems are set up per clinic during onboarding. Tell us which system you use when you book your demo.

Can patients always reach a human?

Yes. Patients can ask for a person at any time. During office hours the call is transferred to your front desk. After hours a priority callback task is created with the full context.

Is patient information secure?

Sensitive fields are encrypted, access is role-based, every view of patient data is audit-logged, and the assistant only shares information after verifying identity. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How long does setup take?

Most clinics are ready for a test call the same day: add your hours, providers and appointment types, import your website, then forward your number.
